# Peripherals and Drivers · 5G

The driver has been adapted for the China Mobile MR880A 5G module. The driver code is located in the Robot SDK under `components/peripherals/5g`. To purchase the module, contact the manufacturer directly.

If you need a plug-and-play 5G communication module, you can refer to the "Unlimited 4G China Unicom SuiYiLian 2nd Gen (new) + USB adapter" available on [JD.com](https://item.jd.com/10103248412831.html).
